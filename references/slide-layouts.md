# Dense layout catalogue (consulting style: BCG / MBB)

The right density = **a COMPOSED, multi-zone slide that fills the whole canvas**, not "one exhibit + 3 bullets" nor half-empty prose. Draw on these patterns (modeled on real consulting-firm templates). Each slide = **common chrome** + **2 to 4 coordinated zones** + **"Key takeaway" band**.

## Common chrome (all content slides)
- **Number** + **title** (action title) + **subtitle** (1 line).
- **2 to 4 zones** laid out in a grid (left/right, or 3 columns, or diagram top / table bottom).
- **"Key takeaway" band** at the bottom (the so-what).
- **Source** bottom-left, **page number** (+ logo) bottom-right.
- Golden rule: **fill the usable area**; aim for several coordinated elements, not one object surrounded by white space.

## Reusable components (building blocks)
- **Metric cards**: row of 3-5 boxes "big number + label" (economic impact, summary KPIs).
- **Harvey balls** ●◐○: qualitative rating (trade-offs, validation, strength) on a 0-4 scale.
- **Attractiveness dots** ●●●: 1-3 or 1-5 rating (segments, capabilities).
- **Dense table**: 4-8 rows, key column highlighted, anthracite header.
- **Waterfall / bridge**: profit pool, EBITDA bridge, margin.
- **2×2**: matrix (attractiveness × strength, growth × share) with positioned items.
- **Issue tree**: problem → branches → sub-branches.
- **Value chain chevrons**: steps as arrows + activity/cost/pain-point rows.
- **Gantt / roadmap**: bars per phase on a time axis + milestones (diamonds).
- **Cycle diagram**: operating model (center + 4-5 satellites).
- **Positioning scatter**: players placed on 2 axes.
- **Venn / framework**: 3 circles (needs, attractiveness, access).

## The patterns (by use case) — each = a dense, multi-zone layout

### Framing & synthesis
1. **Context & ambition**: left col. "Context" (3-4 fleshed-out bullets); right col. "Key strategic questions" (numbered); inset "Our ambition" + "Constraints" (icons).
2. **Executive synthesis (impact logic)**: "Key messages" (3, fleshed-out bullets) | **economic impact** inset (3 metric cards: +X M€, +Y pts, +Z %); **impact chain** (4 steps as arrows); **bottom line** in a band.
3. **Executive summary (S-C-R)**: column **Situation / Complication / Question / Recommendation / Financial impact** (icons + text) | **impact inset** (table: Revenue uplift, EBITDA, Payback, ROI).

### Market
4. **Market overview**: **bars** (market size, CAGR) + **donut** (segmentation) + takeaway band.
5. **Market evolution / tipping points**: table **Past / Present / Next horizon** per dimension (growth, customer, regulation) | numbered list of **tipping points**.
6. **Profit pool / where the value goes**: **waterfall** (pool size per stage) on top | **table** "segment: pool N, pool N+1, change, trend (arrow), drivers" below.

### Customers & competition
7. **Segmentation**: **Venn framework** (needs / attractiveness / access) | **segment table** (size, growth, **attractiveness dots**) | implications.
8. **Competitive landscape**: **player table** (revenue, margin, share) | **positioning scatter** (offering × strength) | takeaway.
9. **Competitive advantage**: **2×2** (differentiation × strength) with "where we win" zones | "our advantages" list (icons).

### Diagnosis
10. **Value chain**: **chevrons** (links) + **activities / cost structure (%) / pain points / opportunities** rows.
11. **Issue tree**: breakdown problem → levers → sub-levers, with supporting text on the right.
12. **Root cause analysis**: **table** "problem · possible causes · evidence · validation (**harvey balls**) · root cause" + legend.
13. **Benchmarking**: **KPI table** (company, top quartile, median, bottom, **gap to top**) + takeaway.

### Strategy & options
14. **Growth-share matrix (portfolio)**: **2×2** (stars/qm/cash cows/pets) + **portfolio summary** (metric cards: no. of businesses, total revenue, total EBITDA, margin).
15. **Options architecture**: **columns per option** (description) + **trade-off table** (potential, profitability, risk… as **harvey balls**) + **our read**.
16. **Scenarios**: **case table** (base / high / low: CAGR, revenue, margin) + **key assumptions** per case + implications.
17. **Capability assessment**: **table** (capability · current maturity · required · gap · priority · actions) with **dots**.

### Finance
18. **Financial performance**: **revenue bars** + **margin bars/line** side by side + comments.
19. **Profitability bridge**: **waterfall** (EBITDA N → volume/mix, price, COGS, SG&A → EBITDA N+1) + margin %.
20. **Value creation levers**: **large table** (lever · opportunity € · impact · feasibility · lead time · enablers) + total.
21. **Investment case**: **financial table** (Revenue, EBITDA, Capex, FCF, ROI: base/projected/delta) + **assumptions** + **key risks** + verdict.

### Execution
22. **Operating model**: **cycle diagram** (customer impact at the center + organization/governance/process/tech around it) + **key changes** (from X → to Y).
23. **Transformation roadmap**: **Gantt** by phases (Mobilize/Build/Scale/Optimize) on a time axis + **milestones** (diamonds) + initiative rows.
24. **Initiatives view**: **table** (initiative · description · investment · revenue/EBITDA impact · timing · owner) + total.
25. **Decision agenda / Next steps**: **key decisions** (numbered) | **no-regret moves (0-90 d)** | **next steps** | **expected impact** band (metric cards).

## Program governance / PMO (delivery register — very dense, tabular)
**RAG** status everywhere (green/amber/red = **functional** colors, an accepted exception to "a single accent"). Reporting period at the top.
26. **Control tower**: **overall status** (RAG dot + sentence) | **KPI row** (Scope/Schedule/Budget/Quality in %) | **key milestones** (planned/actual/status table) | **top risks** (level) | **decisions to take**.
27. **Executive status dashboard**: overall RAG status + **key message** + **decisions required** (counter) | **workstream table** (RAG status, % progress, this week, next week, blockers, owner) | **escalations**.
28. **Workstream progress tracker**: **large table** per workstream with sub-columns Scope / Plan / Delivery (**harvey balls** or %), Risks, Dependencies, Next milestone, PMO comment; RAG legend (≥90% / 70-89% / <70%).
29. **Integrated plan (Gantt)**: **activity bars** per workstream on a weeks/months axis + **dependencies** (arrows) + **milestones** (diamonds) + "today" marker.
30. **Milestone achievement**: **table** planned vs. actual (date, RAG status, impact, recovery action) + **slippage bars** (days of drift) + critical milestone list.
31. **RAID log**: 4 compact tables **Risks / Assumptions / Issues / Dependencies** (id, description, severity, owner, age) + escalations.
32. **Decisions required**: **table** (decision, options, recommendation, impact if not decided, required by, owner) + **decisions taken** since the last committee.
33. **Dependency map**: **matrix/heatmap** workstream × workstream (strong/medium/none) + top dependencies + "blocked by".
34. **Budget burn**: **cumulative curve** planned/actual/forecast + **budget table** (per workstream: budget, actual, forecast, variance, % consumed) + financial comment.
35. **Resource & capacity**: **table** of roles (FTE required vs. available, gap, risk) + **load heatmap** (role × weeks) + critical gaps + actions.
36. **Scope change control**: pipeline **metric cards** (total/approved/in review/rejected/impacting) + **CR table** (description, origin, cost/effort/schedule impact, status) + critical summary.
37. **Sprint / next 30 days**: **metric cards** (tests, pass rate, defects, criticals) + **defect trend** (curve) + top defects (table) + **release readiness** (RAG).
38. **Change adoption**: training progress + **adoption heatmap** (BU × readiness/adoption/resistance) + audience readiness table.
39. **Value realization**: **metric cards** (target / secured / realized / forecast) + **table per initiative** (target value, realized, confidence, timing, evidence) + actions.

**PMO components (building blocks)**: **RAG dot** (green #2E7D32 / amber #E68A00 / red #C62828, functional), **heatmap** (grid of cells colored green→red), **harvey balls**/%, **Gantt** (bars + diamonds + dependencies), **burn curve**, **counters/status badges**.

## Thought leadership / point of view (illustrated strategy)
40. **Key messages**: 3 numbered cards (01/02/03, bold message + explanation) + **"So what?"** inset on the right.
41. **Big picture (concentric layers)**: **nested** ovals/circles (macro → sector → company priorities → value creation), labels per layer + framing text on the left.
42. **Icon columns** (Trends to watch / Strategic imperatives): **3 to 5 columns**, each = **line icon** + title + 2-3 lines; synthesis band at the bottom. (Icons allowed here, see note.)
43. **How to win (formula)**: sequence of **circles** connected by **+** and **=** (insight + capabilities + model = advantage) + **"success factors"** row (icon + label).
44. **Pull-quote (point of view)**: analysis text on the left + **quote box** (large sentence, accent border) on the right — the strong message.
45. **Case in point / results**: case text on the left + **quantified results with deltas** (+28%, −20%, 2.5×) on the right, icon + label.
46. **Chevron roadmap**: 4 horizontal **phases as arrows** (title + horizon) + action sub-lists per phase. (Variant of the Gantt roadmap #23.)
47. **Action insets**: **"So what?"**, **"What we need from you"**, **"Next steps"** = small labeled boxes (light fill + accent bar) to place in the margin.

### Note on ICONS (coded)
**Sober line icons** are **allowed** in the column/imperatives/success-factor patterns and in insets — **monochrome** (accent or gray), **one consistent set**, **one per item**. Forbidden: icons on analytical exhibits (tables/charts), decorative icons with no meaning, mixed styles, emoji as replacements.
Helper provided: **`assets/icon.cjs`** (`makeIcon(name, colorHex, outDir)` → monochrome PNG from **react-icons**), to pass to `iconColumns({icon: path})`. E.g. `await makeIcon('FaChartLine', accentInk, 'out/icons')`.

## Density rules (reminder)
- **Multi-zone**: nearly every slide combines 2-3 elements (e.g. diagram + table, table + scatter, waterfall + table). In consulting, **several exhibits per slide** are the norm (≠ the academic "one only" rule).
- **Fill the canvas**: no large voids; if a zone is thin, enrich it (evidence, drivers, a second exhibit) or rebalance the grid.
- **Key takeaway** at the bottom of every content slide.
- **Still legible**: clear hierarchy, a single accent, do not pile up to the point of illegibility (if too much → split into 2 slides, both full).
