# "Light keynote" register (historical V1 style, KEPT)

Style guide from the builder's first generation, **kept as an optional register**. Choose it at **brief reading** when the deck is light, marketing-oriented, heavily presented, or aimed at a general audience (keynote, visual pitch, inspiration deck): in that case, these rules take precedence over
the Sprint OS density floors. The V3 invariants ALWAYS remain in force: never an em dash,
sourced numbers, a single accent color, visual QA, bold on key ideas.

## Before starting
- **Bold, subject-tied palette**: the palette must feel designed for THIS subject. If it would work
  as-is for a completely different deck, it is not specific enough.
- **Dominance rather than equality**: one color dominates (60-70% of visual weight), 1-2 supporting tones,
  one sharp accent. Never all colors at equal weight.
- **Dark/light contrast**: dark backgrounds for title + conclusion, light for content (the "sandwich"),
  or a deliberate all-dark for a premium look.
- **One repeated visual motif**: pick ONE distinctive element (rounded image frames, icons in colored
  circles) and carry it across every slide. Never an accent bar/band as the motif.

## For every slide (keynote register)
**Every slide carries a visual element**: image, chart, icon, or shape. All-text slides are
to be avoided in THIS register (unlike the Sprint OS register, where dense prose reigns).

**Layouts**: two columns (text left, visual right); icon + text rows (icon in a colored
circle, bold title, description below); 2×2 or 2×3 grid (image on one side, content blocks on the
other); half-bleed image (full left or right half) with content overlaid.

**Data display**: big featured numbers (60-72 pt with a small label below); comparison
columns (before/after, pros/cons); timeline or numbered flow with arrows.

**Finishing touches**: icons in small colored circles near section headers; accent italics
for key stats or taglines.

## Typography (keynote register)
| Element | Size |
|---------|--------|
| Slide title | 36-44 pt bold |
| Section header | 20-24 pt bold |
| Body | 14-16 pt |
| Captions | 10-12 pt muted |

## Spacing
- Margins min. 0.5 in; 0.3-0.5 in between content blocks; let it breathe, do not fill everything
  (this is the AIRY register: the deliberate opposite of the Sprint OS density floors).

## Avoid (original list, still in force)
- Do not repeat the same layout on every slide; do not center body text.
- Titles clearly larger than body; no generic default blue.
- Consistent spacing (0.3 OR 0.5, not a mix); style the whole deck or nothing.
- No all-text slides; no low-contrast elements.
- **Never accent lines under titles nor decorative bars/bands** (AI tells).
- No cream/beige backgrounds by default; never text overflowing its box.

## When to choose this register over Sprint OS
| Brief signal | Register |
|---|---|
| Working deck, board, steering committee (COPIL), study, analysis | **Sprint OS** (default) |
| Keynote, launch, inspiration deck, general-public communication, highly visual pitch | **Light keynote (this file)** |
| Mixed (e.g. investor pitch) | Sprint OS for the evidence, keynote touches on the highlight moments |
